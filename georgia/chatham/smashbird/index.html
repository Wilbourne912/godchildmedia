Our notes · Written by a person

Smashbird is a fast food restaurant at 13051 Abercorn St, Suite B5, in Savannah's Wilshire Estates area. It holds a 4.8 rating across 186 reviews as of October 2026.

What the records show

Smashbird operates in a shopping plaza on a parcel zoned for neighborhood and planned business use. The plaza was built in 1989. The state registry lists the business as Smashbird Co., and the city has it on a business tax certificate. It also holds a Class C retail license for beer and wine consumption.

Its health record shows 100 out of 100, Grade A. That comes from a late-summer 2026 evaluation described as a pre-opening clearance, so treat it as a first inspection rather than a track record. We will update it as routine inspections appear.

Part of a larger group

Smashbird belongs to Next Bite Restaurant Group, which also operates Savannah Smokehouse BBQ & Brew downtown.

Access, hours and contact

Two wheelchair accessibility items are on record as accessible, and the others are unknown, which is not the same as no. Hours as listed: Monday to Thursday 11 AM to 9 PM, Friday and Saturday 11 AM to 10 PM, Sunday noon to 9 PM. Call (912) 231-3543, visit smashbirdcompany.com, or find it on Instagram at @smashbirdco. Hours change, so confirm before you go.
